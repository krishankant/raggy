Service centres

Besides our own workshops in Merrow and Fenwick, three partner shops are approved Brightwater service centres. They can carry out servicing and warranty inspections, but they cannot sell bikes or take returns.

Saltmarsh Cycles, Port Edda

Open Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 17:30. Phone 0161 555 0142. Saltmarsh is the only service centre that works on the Ebb E-Cargo's motor and battery outside our own workshops.

The Spoke Room, Calder

Open Wednesday to Sunday, 10:00 to 19:00. Phone 0161 555 0178. The Spoke Room also offers same-day puncture repair for any bike, Brightwater or not, for 12 credits.

Northgate Wheels, Arlen Islands

Open Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday, 9:00 to 13:00. Phone 0161 555 0193. Customers on the Arlen Islands can drop off warranty claims here instead of shipping the bike to the mainland.

Booking a service

A standard service costs 65 credits and a full service costs 140 credits at every centre, including our own workshops. A full service includes new brake pads and a new chain; a standard service includes adjustment only. Book by phone with the centre, or online for our own workshops. The first standard service within six months of purchase is free.
