Warranty

Brightwater frames carry a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects for the original owner. Forks are covered for five years. Components made by other companies (brakes, gears, tyres) carry their maker's warranty, usually two years.

The Ebb E-Cargo battery is covered for three years or 800 full charge cycles, whichever comes first. A battery that holds less than 70% of its original capacity inside that period is replaced free of charge.

The warranty does not cover crash damage, wear parts (chains, brake pads, tyres, cables), or damage from racing or commercial use. Commercial use of the Ebb by registered delivery businesses is covered under a separate two-year fleet warranty.

To make a claim, bring the bike to either workshop or email warranty@brightwater.example with photos and your order number. Most claims are decided within five working days.

As a goodwill gesture, Brightwater replaces a frame damaged in a crash at 40% off the list price, once per owner, even though crash damage isn't covered.
