Bike fitting and sizing

Choosing a frame size

Measure your inside leg (floor to crotch, in socks) and use this guide for the Heron and Kestrel frames:

  • XS: inside leg 68 to 73 cm (Kestrel only)
  • S: 72 to 77 cm
  • M: 76 to 81 cm
  • L: 80 to 85 cm
  • XL: 84 to 90 cm

If you fall between two sizes, choose the smaller size for a more upright, relaxed position and the larger size for a stretched, faster position.

The Wren Kids 20

The Wren suits children with an inside leg of 50 to 60 cm. Most children outgrow it at around 9 years old.

Professional fitting

A professional bike fit takes 90 minutes and costs 120 credits. It is free with any new bike costing over 1,500 credits if booked within 30 days of delivery. Fittings are done only at the Merrow workshop, on Fridays and Saturdays.

Size exchanges

If a new bike turns out to be the wrong size, you can exchange it for another size of the same model within 30 days, even if it has been ridden more than 10 km, as long as it is undamaged. Size exchanges are free of charge.
