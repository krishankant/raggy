Brightwater Bikes: our story

Brightwater Bikes is a fictional bicycle cooperative used as sample data for the raggy course. Nothing here describes a real company.

Brightwater was founded in 2011 by Ines Okafor and Tomas Rehn in a converted boathouse on the Merrow Canal. The first shop had two repair stands and a rack of rebuilt commuter bikes. Ines had spent nine years as a frame builder; Tomas ran the books and, by his own account, mostly made tea.

In 2015 the shop became a worker cooperative. Every employee who completes twelve months of service may buy one member share for 250 credits, which gives one vote at the annual general meeting. The cooperative now has 34 member-owners.

The company opened a second workshop in Fenwick in 2019 and launched its own frame line, the Heron, in 2021. Brightwater's head office remains at the original boathouse, 4 Lock Lane, Merrow.

Brightwater's mascot is a grey heron called Pip, which is why the first frame line carries the bird's name.
