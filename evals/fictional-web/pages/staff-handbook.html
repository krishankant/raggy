Staff handbook (excerpt)

Hours

Both workshops open Tuesday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00. They close on Sundays and Mondays. On the first Thursday of each month the Merrow workshop stays open until 21:00 for the free community repair evening.

Community repair evening

Anyone can bring a bike and fix it with our tools and guidance, at no cost. Parts are sold at cost price. Two staff mechanics are on duty and the evening is capped at 20 bikes.

Staff discount

Staff get 30% off accessories and 20% off bikes after their probation period of three months. Member-owners get an extra 5% on bikes.

Mechanic levels

Mechanics progress through three levels: Apprentice, Mechanic, and Master Mechanic. Becoming a Master Mechanic requires at least four years in the workshop and passing the wheel-building assessment, which involves building two wheels that stay true within 0.5 mm after a 200 km test ride.

Safety

Eye protection is required when using the frame-facing tools. The workshop first-aid kit is kept beside the truing stand, and the fire assembly point is the towpath gate.
