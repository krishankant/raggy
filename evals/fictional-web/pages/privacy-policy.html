Privacy policy (summary)

Brightwater Bikes stores your name, delivery address, email address and order history so we can deliver orders, handle returns and honour warranties.

We keep order records for seven years because tax law requires it. Marketing preferences and newsletter data are deleted within 30 days of you unsubscribing.

We never sell personal data. We share your address with our courier only for delivery, and your bike's frame number with the national bike register only if you opt in when you buy.

You can ask for a copy of everything we hold about you by emailing privacy@brightwater.example. We answer within one month. You can also ask us to correct or delete your data, except records we must keep by law.

Our website uses one essential cookie for the shopping basket. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Our data protection officer is Mara Lindqvist.
