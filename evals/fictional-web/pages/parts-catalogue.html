The BW-BT-5000 battery can only be fitted by a workshop or by Saltmarsh Cycles; it is not sold for home fitting.

Parts marked "BW" are covered by our two-year parts warranty. Parts marked "XP" carry their maker's warranty.

Parts orders ship with standard accessory delivery. Out-of-stock parts are usually back within three weeks; we email you when they arrive.
