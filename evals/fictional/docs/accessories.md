# Accessories

## Racks and bags
The Heron rear rack (65 credits) carries up to 25 kg and fits both Heron models. The Kestrel cannot take a rear rack because its frame has no rack mounts; for the Kestrel we recommend the 9-litre frame bag (48 credits).

## Child seats
The Ebb E-Cargo box seats two children with its built-in harnesses. For other bikes we sell the Nestling rear child seat (110 credits) for children from 9 months to 22 kg. The Nestling fits the Heron Commuter and Heron Tourer only.

## Locks
We recommend the Merrow D-lock (52 credits), rated Gold by the Cycle Security Board. Every new bike comes with a free cable lock, which is meant for short stops only.

## Helmets
Helmets cannot be returned once the hygiene seal is broken. Our helmets come in three sizes: S (51 to 55 cm), M (55 to 59 cm) and L (59 to 63 cm).

## Lights
The Heron Commuter has dynamo lights built in. For every other model we sell the Lumen 400 rechargeable light set (36 credits), which charges by USB-C and lasts about 6 hours on its steady setting.
