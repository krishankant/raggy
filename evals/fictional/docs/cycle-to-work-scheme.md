# Cycle-to-work scheme

Brightwater takes part in the national Ride to Work scheme, which lets employees spread the cost of a bike over salary deductions before tax.

## Who can use it
Your employer must be registered with the scheme. You need to be 18 or over and on a permanent or fixed-term contract lasting at least 12 months.

## Limits
The scheme covers bikes and safety equipment up to 2,500 credits in total, so every model except the Ebb E-Cargo qualifies on its own. The Ebb (4,950 credits) can only be bought through the scheme if your employer has raised its limit; ask your employer before ordering.

## How it works
1. Ask your employer for a scheme certificate.
2. Choose your bike and accessories in either workshop and show the certificate.
3. We invoice your employer directly; you collect the bike within 14 days of the invoice being paid.

Bikes bought through the scheme can't be returned for a refund, but the usual warranty and the free size exchange still apply.
