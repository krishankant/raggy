# The Brightwater range (2026)

## Heron Commuter
A steel city bike with an 8-speed hub gear, hub dynamo lights and full mudguards. Frame sizes: S, M, L, XL. Weight: 13.9 kg. Price: 1,180 credits.

## Heron Tourer
A long-distance touring bike with a 2x10 drivetrain, three bottle mounts and rack mounts front and rear. It can carry up to 40 kg of luggage. Weight: 14.6 kg. Price: 1,640 credits.

## Kestrel Gravel
An aluminium gravel bike with 45 mm tyres and hydraulic disc brakes. Frame sizes: XS to XL. Weight: 10.2 kg. Price: 1,390 credits. The Kestrel is the only Brightwater model sold in an XS size.

## Wren Kids 20
A 20-inch children's bike for riders roughly 6 to 9 years old, with a single-speed drivetrain and a coaster brake. Weight: 8.1 kg. Price: 340 credits.

## Ebb E-Cargo
A front-loading electric cargo bike with a 500 Wh battery, a range of 50 to 90 km depending on load, and a box that seats two children. Weight: 38 kg. Price: 4,950 credits. The Ebb is assembled at the Fenwick workshop only.
