# Returns and refunds

You can return any unused bike or accessory within 30 days of delivery. "Unused" means ridden less than 10 km, with no damage and all original packaging.

To start a return, email returns@brightwater.example with your order number. We reply within two working days with a return label. Bikes must travel in the original box; if you no longer have it, we can send a replacement box for 25 credits.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 14 days of the return arriving at our warehouse. Delivery charges are refunded only when the item was faulty or we sent the wrong item.

Custom-built Heron frames are made to order and cannot be returned unless they are faulty. Gift cards cannot be refunded.

If you return a bike after 30 days but within 60 days, we offer store credit instead of a refund, minus a 10% restocking fee.
