# Shipping and delivery

We deliver to addresses on the mainland and the Arlen Islands. Bikes ship fully built from the Merrow warehouse except the Ebb E-Cargo, which ships from Fenwick.

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days and costs 35 credits for a bike and 6 credits for accessories. Orders over 900 credits ship free. Delivery to the Arlen Islands takes 7 to 10 working days and always costs 60 credits, even on orders over 900 credits.

Express delivery (next working day) is available for accessories only, for 15 credits, on orders placed before 1 pm.

Every bike delivery needs a signature. If nobody is home, the courier tries twice more and then returns the bike to the warehouse; redelivery after that costs 20 credits.

You can also collect any order for free from either workshop. Collection orders are ready within two working days, and we hold them for 21 days.
